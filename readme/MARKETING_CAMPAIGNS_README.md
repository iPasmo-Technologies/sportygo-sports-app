Use `https://app.sportygo.com.sg` as the public app URL. Use the tagged platform links in the Measurement section when publishing campaigns so visits can be attributed to their source.

## 2. Launch Positioning

### Public value proposition

SportyGo is the all-in-one sports booking app built to make getting active simpler. Players can explore available facilities, choose a date and time, complete card payment, receive a booking confirmation, and manage upcoming bookings without switching between calls, chats, spreadsheets, and payment messages.

### Launch-ready highlights

- Cricket, indoor cricket, and pickleball facility booking
- Indoor and outdoor facility choices at the current SportyGo venue
- Date and time-slot availability in 30-minute intervals
- Flexible booking duration based on consecutive available slots
- Secure credit or debit card checkout powered by Stripe
- Booking confirmation with a unique booking ID
- Email confirmations and booking updates when email delivery is configured
- Upcoming and previous booking history
- Booking rescheduling and cancellation
- Venue address and map access
- Installable web app for quick Home Screen access on supported devices

### Core audiences

1. **Friends and social groups:** People arranging weekend cricket or pickleball games.
2. **Working adults:** Players who want to secure evening or weekend sessions quickly.
3. **Parents and young athletes:** Families looking for convenient practice facilities.
4. **Clubs and community groups:** Organisers who repeatedly coordinate sports sessions.
5. **New and returning players:** People looking for a simple way to start playing again.

### Message pillars

- **Convenience:** The booking journey is together in one app.
- **Clarity:** See facility information, prices, dates, and available times before checkout.
- **Confidence:** Get a clear booking record and confirmation ID.
- **Control:** View, reschedule, or cancel eligible upcoming bookings.
- **Community:** Spend less time organising and more time playing together.

## 9. Campaign Ideas That Can Grow

### "From Chat to Match" challenge

Ask groups to show their messy planning chat followed by their SportyGo booking confirmation. Feature the best transformation as a Reel or Short, with permission and all personal details hidden.

### "Who Organises the Game?" appreciation campaign

Invite followers to tag the person who always finds the venue, collects money, and confirms attendance. Position SportyGo as the organiser's new shortcut.

### "Try a New Sport" series

Start with cricket and pickleball. Publish beginner-friendly videos, facility tours, and simple first-session checklists. Expand the series only when each additional sport becomes bookable.

### Community spotlight

Feature real teams, families, clubs, and casual players. Focus each story on why they play and how SportyGo made organising the session easier.

### Off-peak activation

Promote selected quieter sessions with an approved incentive. Examples: weekday practice bundles, first-session offers, or refer-a-friend credits. Publish an offer only after its code, eligibility, expiry, redemption flow, and terms are implemented.

## 10. Creative Direction

- Show the real app and real facilities rather than generic stock imagery.
- Lead videos with the organising problem, then reveal the booking flow.
- Use SportyGo's red, white, gold, and deep navy brand palette consistently.
- Keep the logo visible but let players, courts, nets, and app screens carry the story.
- Film vertical `9:16` for Reels, Stories, and Shorts; use `1:1` or `4:5` for feed posts.
- Add captions to every video so the message works without sound.
- Keep a single CTA per creative: open SportyGo and book.
- Hide names, email addresses, card details, and full booking IDs in demonstrations.

## 11. Measurement

### Ready-to-use campaign links

Use a different tagged link for each platform:

- WhatsApp: `https://app.sportygo.com.sg?utm_source=whatsapp&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=your_game_starts_here`
- Instagram: `https://app.sportygo.com.sg?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=your_game_starts_here`
- YouTube: `https://app.sportygo.com.sg?utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=video&utm_campaign=your_game_starts_here`
- Facebook: `https://app.sportygo.com.sg?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=your_game_starts_here`

For example, publish this complete link in a WhatsApp campaign:

`https://app.sportygo.com.sg?utm_source=whatsapp&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=your_game_starts_here`

The text after `?` does not change the destination page. It supplies campaign labels that an analytics service can read:

- `utm_source=whatsapp`: where the visitor came from
- `utm_medium=organic`: the traffic type, in this case an unpaid post or message
- `utm_campaign=your_game_starts_here`: the campaign shared by all launch channels
- `utm_content`: optional label for different creatives or placements

### How to enable campaign tracking

1. **Choose an analytics platform.** Create a Google Analytics 4 web data stream for `https://app.sportygo.com.sg`, or use the organisation's existing analytics platform.
2. **Install its tracking tag.** Add the analytics tag to the SportyGo frontend and deploy it. A UTM URL works as a link without this step, but visits will not appear in analytics reports until tracking is installed.
3. **Publish the matching tagged URL.** Use the WhatsApp link only on WhatsApp, the Instagram link only on Instagram, and so on.
4. **Test before launch.** Open each link in a private browser window, confirm SportyGo loads, then check the analytics real-time report for the expected source, medium, and campaign.
5. **Review acquisition reports.** In GA4, open **Reports → Acquisition → Traffic acquisition** and use **Session source / medium** or **Session campaign** as the report dimension.
6. **Mark business events.** Track account registration, booking start, checkout start, and completed booking as events. Mark completed booking as a key event so campaigns can be compared by conversions rather than clicks alone.
7. **Keep naming consistent.** Use lowercase values and underscores. Do not rename `your_game_starts_here` during the same campaign.

### Track individual posts

Add `utm_content` when publishing multiple posts on one platform. This shows which creative performed best:

- WhatsApp launch broadcast: `https://app.sportygo.com.sg?utm_source=whatsapp&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=your_game_starts_here&utm_content=launch_broadcast`
- WhatsApp weekend reminder: `https://app.sportygo.com.sg?utm_source=whatsapp&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=your_game_starts_here&utm_content=weekend_reminder`
- Instagram launch Reel: `https://app.sportygo.com.sg?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=your_game_starts_here&utm_content=group_chat_reel`
- Facebook launch post: `https://app.sportygo.com.sg?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=your_game_starts_here&utm_content=launch_post`

Do not use URL shorteners until the full tagged URLs have been tested. Some community groups and messaging clients may distrust unfamiliar shortened domains.

Track these outcomes weekly:

- Link clicks and app visits by platform
- Account registrations
- Users who begin selecting a facility or slot
- Completed bookings
- Visit-to-booking conversion rate
- Cost per completed booking for paid campaigns
- Repeat bookings within 30 days
- Reshares, saves, comments, and tagged teammates

## 12. Public Claim Guardrails

Campaigns must reflect what users can book at publication time.

### Safe launch claims

- "All-in-one sports booking app"
- "Explore facilities, choose available slots, pay by card, and manage bookings"
- "Cricket, indoor cricket, and pickleball booking"
- "Secure card checkout powered by Stripe"
- "Install SportyGo on your Home Screen on supported devices"

### Do not publish yet

- "100+ facilities" or any unverified facility count
- "Across Singapore" when bookings currently use one venue
- "Book badminton, tennis, futsal, basketball, or other coming-soon sports"
- "Join an academy," "book a coach," or "buy sports gear" while those actions are disabled
- "Google Pay," "PayNow," or "GrabPay" while those methods are marked coming soon
- "Split payment" because group payment splitting is not implemented
- "Download on the App Store or Google Play" unless native store listings exist
- "Guaranteed availability" or "instant confirmation" without acknowledging payment and slot completion
- Discounts, referral credits, or promo codes that are not implemented and approved

### Approved wording for future breadth

Use **"Built to bring more of your sports journey into one place"** or **"More sports and experiences are coming"** instead of advertising currently disabled features as available.
